# The Problem

- Finding football matches is difficult.
- Players rely on group chats.
- Matches can be cancelled when players are missing.

---

<!-- Slide 3 -->
# Our Solution

KickMate helps players:

- Find nearby football matches
- Join available matches
- Create their own matches

---

<!-- Slide 4 -->
# User Research

I interviewed a football player.

Main findings:

- Players use WhatsApp to organize games.
- Finding enough available players is difficult.
- Last-minute cancellations cause problems.

---

<!-- Slide 5 -->
# Design Process

1. User research
2. Information architecture
3. Wireframes
4. Clickable prototype
5. Usability testing
6. Design improvements

---

<!-- Slide 6 -->
# Main Screens

- Home: discover matches
- Search: find available games
- Match Details: check information
- Create Match: organize a game
- Success Screen: confirm joining

---

<!-- Slide 7 -->
# Usability Testing

I tested the prototype with users.

- Users could find matches.
- One user encountered a full match.
- Feedback helped identify improvements.

---

<!-- Slide 8 -->
# Improvements

- Clearer navigation
- Better match information
- Simpler match creation
- Reusable buttons and components

---

<!-- Slide 9 -->
# Final Prototype

KickMate includes:

- Match discovery
- Match details
- Joining matches
- Creating matches
- Success and full-match states

The interactive prototype is available in Figma.
